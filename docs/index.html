Before you install or update through SideStore
  1. In iLoader, confirm the Apple Account says LOGGED IN AS and the iPhone is selected over USB.
  2. Use a built-in provider that has successfully authenticated on this computer. Do not paste the LeeWay update URL into Anisette settings.
  3. Open Certificates. Preserve the existing iOS Development certificate unless iLoader has authenticated and specifically reports an install-stage signing-identity error.
  4. Choose SideStore (Stable).
  5. Open SideStore on the iPhone and complete its own Apple Account sign-in. iLoader's login does not sign in SideStore.

LeeWay records this preflight and keeps its update catalog separate from SideStore authentication. iLoader remains the owner-controlled Apple signer.
