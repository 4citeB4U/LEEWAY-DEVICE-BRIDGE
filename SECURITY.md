# Security

## Owner sovereignty

The device owner is the first operational authority. Platform and legal restrictions remain hard boundaries. LeeWay may be stricter but never weaker.

## Separation

SUPPORTED != AVAILABLE != AUTHORIZED != ACTIVE != HEALTHY != VERIFIED.

Files, screen observation, pointer guidance, UI control, app interoperability, clipboard, shell and diagnostics are independently authorized.

## Protected operations

Deletion, security changes, credentials, payments, permission grants, account changes and destructive recovery require elevated policy and may require direct human confirmation.

## Data minimization

Receipts should prove what occurred without persisting private screen content, messages, photos, credentials or filenames unless evidence requirements explicitly justify it.

## Emergency stop

A local STOP AGENT ACCESS action must invalidate active assist-session authority and stop screen/control operations without deleting evidence.
